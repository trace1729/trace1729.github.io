    【中英】尼采对基督教的批判-Dr·MichaelSugrue224

    Friedrich Nietzsche is one of the most important philosophers in the tradition of Western thought. He's one of the most artistic and ruthless and fascinating individuals in the intellectual history of the West. He viewed himself as being the end of the Western intellectual tradition. He saw himself as the destroyer of metaphysics. He saw himself as the destroyer of Christianity.

    弗里德里希·尼采是西方思想传统中最重要的哲学家之一。他是西方思想史上最具艺术性、最无情、最迷人的人物之一。他视自己为西方智识传统的终结者，视自己为形而上学的摧毁者，视自己为基督教的摧毁者。

    He saw himself as a combination philosopher-artist in the tradition of Plato, and he offered a comprehensive critique of the Western intellectual tradition, not conceived merely or narrowly as a philosophical tradition, but as a cultural tradition. Nietzsche, more than anyone else, was a philosopher of culture.

    他视自己为柏拉图传统中哲学家与艺术家的结合体，并对西方智识传统提出了全面的批判——这种传统不仅被狭义地理解为哲学传统，更是作为一种文化传统。尼采比任何人都更是一位文化哲学家。

    He offered criticism of every element in Western culture, and his criticism is noteworthy for its refusal to compromise, for his insistence that we get to the psychological heart of things. What I'd like to talk about in this lecture is Nietzsche's treatment of Christianity and morality, two of the central questions for most philosophers in the Western tradition, and particularly important for Nietzsche because...

    他对西方文化的每一个要素都提出了批判，而他的批判之所以引人注目，在于其拒绝妥协，坚持要触及事物的心理核心。在本次讲座中，我想讨论的是尼采对基督教和道德的处理，这两者是西方传统中大多数哲学家的核心问题，对尼采而言尤其重要，因为……

    Although he is something of a megalomaniac, there's a certain amount of truth to the argument that he is, in fact, the Antichrist. He, in fact, called himself the Antichrist more than once, and he wrote a book called The Antichrist. And what he hoped to do was to get beyond Christianity. He quite literally thought that he was going to supplant Jesus in the Western tradition, which is quite a tall order. Not many people have that sort of ambition.

    尽管他有些自大狂，但说他事实上就是敌基督者，这种说法也有一定道理。他确实不止一次自称敌基督者，还写了一本名为《敌基督者》的书。他希望做的是超越基督教。他确实认为自己将在西方传统中取代耶稣，这可不是一般的雄心壮志。没有多少人会有这样的抱负。

    But Nietzsche was never a shrinking violet, and in fact, he wanted to offer a new set of values. He wished to offer a new cultural orientation to the West, or perhaps to revive an old cultural orientation in the West. And he wished to make us confront the half-truths, the lies, the evasions that are characteristic of almost everyone's life and thought.

    但尼采从来不是个羞怯的人，事实上，他想提供一套新的价值体系。他希望为西方提供一种新的文化取向，或者说是复兴西方一种古老的文化取向。他希望让我们直面那些半真半假的陈述、谎言和逃避，这些几乎充斥在每个人的生活和思想中。

    Now, he wrote several books that criticized morality. In some respects, the tradition of Western ethics is his main target. And perhaps the most intriguing of these books, or one of the most intriguing of these books, is called Beyond Good and Evil. Now, getting beyond good and evil is a rather unusual activity for a philosopher, particularly one concerned with morals. But Nietzsche wants to take...

    他写了好几本批判道德的书。在某些方面，西方伦理学的传统是他的主要靶子。这些书中或许最引人入胜的一本叫做《善恶的彼岸》。对于一位哲学家，尤其是关注道德的哲学家来说，超越善恶是一种相当不寻常的活动。但尼采想要采取……

    an extremely skeptical and extremely naturalistic approach to moral theory and moral valuation, and he wishes to offer a criticism of Christianity and the values that it represents, and in addition, to supplant that with a new code of morals. Nietzsche asks an important question.

    一种极其怀疑和极其自然主义的方法来对待道德理论和道德评价，他希望批判基督教及其所代表的价值观，并在此基础上用一套新的道德规范取而代之。尼采提出了一个重要的问题。

    question that everyone living in this modern age, this one world, anti-metaphysical age, has to confront, which is, where does Christianity and its moral values come from? How is it that human beings conceived of as animals, as featherless bipeds, how is it that they come to have a thing called a conscience?

    这个问题是每个生活在现代、这个一元世界、反形而上学时代的人都必须面对的，那就是：基督教及其道德价值观从何而来？人类，被视为动物、被视为无羽两足动物，他们是如何拥有一种叫做良知的东西的？

    How is it that they make judgments of good and evil, which is not characteristic of fish or birds or reptiles? What is it about human beings which causes them to construct moral systems, which are in some respects the antithesis of nature? Why is it that we feel the desire to conflict with nature and make judgments that nature never does? Nietzsche thinks that he finds the answer in the psyche of the human individual.

    他们如何做出善恶判断，而鱼、鸟或爬行动物却没有这种特征？人类身上有什么东西导致他们构建道德体系，而这些体系在某些方面与自然背道而驰？为什么我们会感到一种与自然冲突、做出自然从不做出的判断的欲望？尼采认为他在人类个体的心灵中找到了答案。

    And he thinks that we are very peculiar animals, animals with strange attributes which are natural and which are part of the human condition. But in fact, Nietzsche says that most of our elevation of ourselves beyond nature, beyond simple facticity,

    他认为我们是非常奇特的动物，拥有一些奇怪的特质，这些特质是自然的，是人类境况的一部分。但事实上，尼采说，我们大多数将自己提升到自然之上、超越简单事实性的做法，

    is a kind of spurious raising of ourselves outside of and above nature. The German term for this is Sonderstellung. What that means is other position, a position somehow outside of nature. Nietzsche wants us to confront the fact that we are animals, that we are part of nature, and that our moral judgments are in fact part of that natural process. In looking at Christianity, Nietzsche goes back to the actual history of Christianity and asks, what is it that Christian morals represent?

    是一种虚假的自我提升，将自己置于自然之外和之上。德语术语是“Sonderstellung”，意思是“特殊位置”，即某种在自然之外的位置。尼采希望我们正视这样一个事实：我们是动物，是自然的一部分，我们的道德判断实际上是那个自然过程的一部分。在审视基督教时，尼采回溯到基督教的真实历史，并问道：基督教道德代表的是什么？

    Whose interests do they serve? And why are these interests disguised? What is it about the moral judgments characteristic of Christianity that needs to be disguised? What is it that, in other words, that's a lie in Christian morals? Remarkable question. Nietzsche's answer is that there are two kinds of morality, and Christianity only represents one particular perspective on judgments of good and evil.

    它们服务于谁的利益？为什么这些利益被伪装起来？基督教特有的道德判断有什么需要伪装的地方？换句话说，基督教道德中的谎言是什么？这是一个非凡的问题。尼采的回答是，有两种道德，而基督教只代表了善恶判断的某一种特定视角。

    And this perspective is the perspective of the herd. Nietzsche contrasts two kinds of morals. Herd morals, morals that are characteristic of the weak, the feeble, the inferior, the enslaved, which is to say Christianity, and master morals, morals of warriors, the predatory human beings who make judgments on the basis of their strength rather than their weakness.

    这种视角是群氓的视角。尼采对比了两种道德：群氓道德，即弱者、卑贱者、劣等者、被奴役者所特有的道德，也就是基督教；以及主人道德，即战士、掠夺性人类的道德，他们基于自己的力量而非软弱做出判断。

    Nietzsche wishes to supplant the herd morals characteristic of Christianity, characteristic of Western culture for the previous 2,000 years, with a new morals, a morals that harkens back to the age of the Homeric hero, which harkens back to the aristocratic warrior elite, which is found all over the world, which creates values out of nothing, and which glories in the fact that their judgments can be enforced in this world, independent of any...

    尼采希望用新的道德取代基督教和过去两千年西方文化所特有的群氓道德，这种新道德回溯到荷马英雄时代，回溯到世界各地的贵族战士精英，他们从无中创造价值，并荣耀于他们的判断可以在现世得到执行，而不依赖于任何……

    metaphysical construct. Now Nietzsche asks, who was it that created Christianity? Well, it's an outgrowth of Judaism and Judaism is a priestly religion. Christianity is an extension of that and Christianity in its earliest historical phases appeals to the slave class in Rome. In fact, Christianity is the revolt of the oppressed masses in Rome.

    形而上学的构造。现在尼采问：是谁创造了基督教？嗯，它是犹太教的产物，而犹太教是一种祭司宗教。基督教是犹太教的延伸，基督教在最早的历史阶段吸引了罗马的奴隶阶级。事实上，基督教是被压迫群众在罗马的反抗。

    And Nietzsche argues that Christianity and its myths was organized so as to articulate the perspective of the slaves, of the inferior, of the feeble. And it was a way of having revenge upon masters, having revenge upon warriors, having revenge upon those who could oppress them, and turning the tables.

    尼采认为，基督教及其神话被组织起来，是为了表达奴隶、卑贱者、弱者的视角。这是一种对主人复仇、对战士复仇、对那些可能压迫他们的人复仇的方式，并扭转了局面。

    by offering a scale of values that was different from and independent of that of the master class. Nietzsche says that the slaves have their revenge and the triumph of Christianity in the West amounts to the triumph of the slave, of the inferior, of the weak and impoverished.

    通过提供一套不同于主人阶级且独立于其价值尺度的价值体系。尼采说，奴隶们的复仇以及基督教在西方世界的胜利，等同于奴隶、劣等人、弱者和穷人的胜利。

    Think about the ideas or the myths that underlie Christianity. The idea that even in this world, if there's no justice, there's another world, heaven and hell, where God's eternal justice gives everyone what they deserve. Nietzsche says that what this in fact is, is the frustration, the impotence, the anger of slaves transformed into a myth.

    想想基督教背后的观念或神话。这种观念认为，即使在这个世界上没有正义，还有另一个世界，即天堂和地狱，在那里上帝的永恒正义会让每个人得到应有的报应。尼采说，这实际上是奴隶们的挫败感、无力和愤怒被转化为一个神话。

    which allows them to get their revenge on those that oppress them, if not in this world, at least in an unreal world, which offers them some solace in a world of pain and misery. Nietzsche contrasts the morality of Christianity with the morality of the Romans. The Romans were fierce, independent, warlike men.

    这使得他们能够向压迫他们的人复仇，即使不在现世，至少在一个虚幻的世界里，这为他们在充满痛苦和悲惨的世界中提供了一些慰藉。尼采将基督教的道德与罗马人的道德进行对比。罗马人是凶猛、独立、好战的民族。

    who made their own scale of values based upon power, based upon the desire to dominate, and they showed no reluctance to inflict pain on those they viewed as inferior. And here inferior means not morally inferior in the Christian sense, but physically inferior in terms of practical strength. Nietzsche's new alternative to Christianity will be a resuscitation of the warlike values of the ancient world. And what he hopes will come out of this...

    他们基于权力和统治欲望建立了自己的价值尺度，并且毫不犹豫地给那些他们认为低劣的人施加痛苦。这里所说的低劣，并非基督教意义上的道德低劣，而是指实际力量上的弱小。尼采对基督教的新替代方案将是对古代世界好战价值观的复兴。而他希望由此产生的……

    will be an evaluation of Christianity which allows us to get beyond Christianity. And the one who is going to succeed Christ as the central figure in Western moral theory will be Nietzsche himself. He frequently signed himself as the Antichrist or as Dionysus, another incarnation of the Nietzschean judgment of good and evil. And in doing this,

    将是对基督教的一种评价，使我们能够超越基督教。而作为西方道德理论核心人物继承基督之位的人，将是尼采本人。他经常自称敌基督者或狄俄尼索斯，这是尼采式善恶判断的另一种化身。通过这样做，

    He raises very disturbing questions about the kind of moral judgments that we make that are still characteristic of this culture and the difficulty of maintaining the traditional judgments of good and evil, characteristic of the West, in an age in which religion and theology are taken less and less seriously.

    他提出了非常令人不安的问题，关于我们所作出的、至今仍具有这种文化特征的道德判断，以及在宗教和神学日益不被重视的时代，维持西方传统善恶判断的困难。

    Now let's think about the idea of getting beyond good and evil. Nietzsche contrasts the slave and the master moralities, and he says what's characteristic of Christianity is the distinction between good and evil. Good, in the Christian sense, means kindness, lovingness, pity, essentially. Nietzsche thinks that Christian morality is a morality of pity, which raises...

    现在让我们思考超越善恶的观念。尼采对比了奴隶道德和主人道德，他说基督教的特征在于区分善与恶。在基督教意义上，善意味着仁慈、仁爱、怜悯。尼采认为基督教道德是一种怜悯的道德，它将……

    to a spurious level of distinction, feeling for the weak, the inferior, and the impoverished. We might contrast this with the master morality, which doesn't distinguish between good and evil. In fact, it distinguishes between good and bad in the sense of capable or incapable, potent or impotent. That's a totally different judgment of value from that characteristic of Christianity. And in fact,

    对弱者、劣者和穷人的同情提升到了一种虚假的卓越地位。我们可以将此与主人道德进行对比，后者并不区分善与恶。事实上，它区分的是好与坏，即有能力与无能力、强大与软弱。这是一种完全不同于基督教的价值判断。事实上，

    This is the scale of values, this master morality, is the one which Nietzsche expects will produce the highest type of human being. Nietzsche holds the view, which at least in part is borrowed from Darwin, that human beings have no intrinsic value. If you remember that one of the great...

    这就是价值尺度，这种主人道德，是尼采期望能产生最高类型人类的道德。尼采持有一种观点，至少部分借鉴自达尔文，即人类没有内在价值。如果你记得其中一个伟大的...

    breakthroughs or one of the great contributions of Christianity to the West is the idea that all souls are equal in the sight of God and that all people are equally valued on some level regardless of what their particular skills or capacities are. Well Nietzsche says that's a mistake. What that is is the revenge of those that can't do anything. And Nietzsche says if we are ever going to produce anything great in this culture, if we are ever going to produce anything significant in this culture, we must get away from the idea that other people really matter. The only elements

    突破或基督教对西方的一大贡献是，所有灵魂在上帝眼中都是平等的，所有人在某种层面上都被平等地重视，无论他们特定的技能或能力如何。尼采说那是个错误。那不过是无能者的报复。尼采说，如果我们想要在这个文化中产生任何伟大的东西，如果我们想要在这个文化中产生任何重要的东西，我们就必须摆脱他人真正重要的观念。人类物种中唯一重要的元素是...

    in the human species which are important are the half dozen great geniuses in any culture. And he says that Christianity inhibits the development of genius. It inhibits the development of creativity.

    任何文化中的少数几个伟大天才。他说基督教抑制了天才的发展，抑制了创造力的发展。

    because it is envious. It is a way of mythologizing the envy of those that are incapable, of those that don't have much in the way of capacity. And Nietzsche says if we are to get beyond that, then we must get beyond this morality of pity. We must get beyond good and evil, back or forward, if you want, to the idea that good and bad are the main standards of our moral judgment. So there will be...

    因为它心怀嫉妒。它是将那些无能者、那些没有多少能力者的嫉妒神话化的一种方式。尼采说，如果我们要超越这一点，我们就必须超越这种怜悯的道德。我们必须超越善恶，回到或前进（如果你愿意）到这样的观念：好与坏是我们道德判断的主要标准。因此将会有...

    None of the conscientiousness characteristic of Christianity, none of the worry about heaven and hell, about God's judgment, this good and bad morality, this master morality will be strictly oriented towards this world. It will not be caught up with the problem of conscience. It will not primarily be concerned.

    基督教特有的那种认真，对天堂地狱、上帝审判的担忧，都不会存在。这种好与坏的道德，这种主人道德，将严格面向此世。它不会纠缠于良心问题。它不会主要关注...

And insofar as we are heeding that Socratic call, Nietzsche, who would be the anti-Socrates, is in fact part of the Socratic tradition as well. The fact that he is both a Socrates and an anti-Socrates is a perfectly Nietzschean oxymoron.

    尼采不号召我们信奉任何教条，而是追求一种更高、更精致的生活。就我们听从这一苏格拉底式的召唤而言，尼采——这个本应成为反苏格拉底的人——实际上也是苏格拉底传统的一部分。他既是苏格拉底又是反苏格拉底，这一事实是一个完美的尼采式矛盾修辞。
